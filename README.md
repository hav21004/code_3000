# code_3000
Collection of Assignments for CSE 3000: Contemporary Issues in Computer Science and Engineering

Ethics are an important part of computing that must be addressed. Machines are not ethical or unethical, they are used ethically. I think that ethics is about making decisions as a developer that will not put bias or ineqaulity into machines. We should develop machines with other people in mind to make sure they are not harmed in any way. We must be aware of the impacts our code can have on other people and strive to reduce the negatives it could cause. My view is that careful thought and planning is important to determine what harm may come from your code, and to make sure you remove anything that may be unethical. Ethics must be considered at all steps of the process.
